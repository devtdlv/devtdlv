## Hi there 👋

# 💫 About Me
Somewhere between Tony Stark’s overconfidence and Monkey D. Luffy’s unstoppable optimism, you’ll find me, a full-stack developer trying to build things that don’t explode (unless they’re supposed to).<br>I spend my days turning ideas into interfaces, bugs into lessons, and caffeine into questionable decisions.<br>I believe good software should feel smooth, fast, and just a little bit magical.<br><br>Right now I’m leveling up my TypeScript and backend wizardry, crafting projects that actually solve real problems instead of sitting in the graveyard of half-finished repos. I’m a big fan of clean architecture, chaotic brainstorms, and any tool that saves time without stealing my soul.<br>If you want to talk code, design, weird bugs, Marvel theories, or why Sanji is criminally underrated, I’m always down.<br><br>My philosophy? Build boldly. Learn constantly. And when in doubt, ask: “What would Iron Man refactor?”
